Answer: 0 | zero

A) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
B) Rabindranath Tagore

A) 26

A) Coal
B) Petrol

D) Diesel

10. Which gas do human beings breathe in to stay alive?
A) Carbon dioxide
B) Nitrogen
C) Oxygen
