1 · Getting started

Who logs in where, and what each person can do.

There is one website and two portals. Which one you see is decided by your account, not by the address you type — students always land in the student portal, teachers in the teacher portal.

PortalWhoWhat they can do
Student Every learner Mark their own attendance, sit exams, see their own report. They cannot see anybody else's records.
Teacher Staff Everything: create batches and exams, edit or correct any record, and see every student's attendance and marks.

Signing in

1

Open the website address your teacher gave you.

2

A student signing in for the first time clicks Create account and enters their name, email, phone number and a password. The password must be at least 8 characters and contain a letter and a number.

3

After that, always use Log in with the same email and password.

Forgotten your password? Click Forgot password on the sign-in screen and enter your email. A reset link is produced. If you do not receive it, your teacher can see the pending request in the teacher portal and pass the link to you. Reset links expire after 60 minutes and work only once.

Before anything else — choose your batch

The very first time a student logs in, they must pick their batch. A batch is the class group, and it carries the timetable: the start and end time and which days of the week the class runs.

This is a one-time choice. Once a batch is chosen it is locked, and only a teacher can change it. This is deliberate — otherwise a student could switch to a later class time to avoid being marked late.

Tab 1 — Attendance

One large button marks you present for today. Beside it is a calendar of the month so you can see your record at a glance.

Tuesday, 28 July 2026 · Class 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Mark now and you are PRESENT. Late after 10:15 AM.

Figure 3. The Attendance tab. The message under the button always tells you what will be recorded if you press it now.

What the button does at different times:

When you clickWhat is recorded
More than 30 min earlyButton is closed. It unlocks 30 minutes before class.
Up to 15 min after start✓Present
After that, before class ends!Late
After class has endedButton is closed. The day counts as ✕Absent
On a non-class dayNothing. Days your batch does not run are never counted against you.

You can only mark once a day. After marking, the button is replaced by what was recorded and at what time. If something is wrong, ask your teacher — only they can change it.

In the calendar beside the button, each day is coloured by what happened, and Saturdays and Sundays are shown as holidays in violet so they are never confused with an absence.

The Attendance tab can be switched off. Your teacher may pause daily marking for a while — during exam week, for example. While it is paused the tab disappears, a notice explains why, and your teacher records attendance for you. Everything else keeps working.

Sitting a paper, step by step:

1

Click the exam. You will see the number of questions, total marks, time per question and the instructions.

2

The Start exam button is greyed out until the exact time your teacher set. It switches on by itself — you do not need to refresh the page.

3

Questions appear one at a time. Use ← Previous and Next →, or the numbered dots, to move about.

4

Each question has its own countdown. It turns amber under 30 seconds and red under 10. When it reaches zero that question locks and cannot be changed — not even by going back to it.

5

On the last question, press Submit exam. Your score appears straight away, and you can open the marked paper to see which answers were right.

Question 3 of 10 · 2 marks
00:24

Which planet is closest to the Sun?

Figure 5. Sitting a paper. The countdown at the top right belongs to this question only.

The timer is enforced by the server, not your browser. Changing the clock on your computer, or pausing the countdown, gains you nothing — an answer that arrives after the deadline is refused and the answer you gave in time is what stands.

If you missed an exam

A paper you never started, which has since closed, is labelled ✕Missed. Open it and you can ask your teacher for a second chance.

  1. Click the missed paper.
  2. Type why you missed it — this is required, and your teacher reads it.
  3. Press Request to write.
  4. The label becomes ⏳Makeup requested while your teacher decides.

A makeup is a different paper. If your teacher agrees, they set a new question paper with a new time, given only to the students who missed the original. You will not get the same questions back. You can ask only once per exam.

Tab 3 — My report

Your own record, and nobody else's. It contains:

  • Attendance breakdown — present, late and absent as a ring chart.
  • Running attendance — a line showing your percentage over time.
  • Month by month — bars comparing each month.
  • Exam results — every paper you have sat, with correct, wrong and unanswered counts, and a Review button to reopen the marked paper.
  • Day by day — the full list of class days and what was recorded.

Use the date boxes, or the Last 7 days / Last 30 days / Last 90 days buttons, to narrow the period. Every chart has a Table view beside it if you prefer numbers to pictures.

Tab 4 — Profile

Your name, email and phone; your batch and its timetable; and Change password. You cannot change your own batch here — ask a teacher.

3 · The teacher portal

Six tabs: Today · Batches · Students · Exams · Reports · Activity

Learner's ByteTeacher portal
Figure 6. The teacher tabs.

Tab 1 — Today

The roll call for today. Every student, their batch, their class time, and what has been recorded so far. Use the search box to find someone by name, email or registration number.

The Set status column lets you override any student's day — use it when a student was in class but could not mark, or when you need to correct a mistake. Your change replaces whatever was there.

Tab 2 — Batches

A batch is a class group and its timetable. Only you can create or change one. Students merely pick which batch they are in.

1

Press + New batch.

2

Give it a name and a course, set the start and end time, and tick the days of the week it runs.

3

Save. Students will now see it when choosing their batch.

Changing a batch time changes it for everyone in it, including how their past days were judged. Set it carefully at the start of a course. A batch you are not running at the moment can be marked inactive instead of deleted, which keeps its records intact.

Tab 3 — Students

Everyone on the system. Search by name, email, phone or registration number, and filter by batch or role. From here you can:

  • Add a person directly, without waiting for them to sign up.
  • Edit their details, or move them to a different batch.
  • Deactivate someone who has left — this keeps their records but stops them logging in.
  • Export CSV to open the whole list in Excel.

Tab 4 — Exams

Creating a paper takes three steps.

1 Exam details 2 Question paper 3 Review & save
Figure 7. The three steps of creating an exam.

Step 1 — Exam details. Choose the batch, give it a title, and set the date, the time it opens, how many questions, the total marks, and how many seconds each question is worth (minimum 5). You can also write your own instructions; leave it blank and sensible ones are generated.

Then choose who the paper is for:

OptionUse it for
Everyone in the batchA normal exam.
Selected students onlyA makeup paper. Only the students you tick will ever see it, so the questions cannot be passed on by classmates who already sat the original.

Step 2 — The question paper. Two ways:

MethodHow it works
✎ Create paper manually Add questions one by one. Each can be fill-in-the-blank or multiple choice. For multiple choice, type the options and mark the correct letter. For fill-in-the-blank, separate several acceptable answers with a | — for example Mumbai|Bombay.
⭱ Upload paper (PDF / Word) Upload a paper that already contains the answers. The questions and the answer key are pulled out automatically. Always check the review screen before saving — it shows what was found and warns about anything it could not read.

Step 3 — Review and save. Check the questions, then Publish. A paper stays a draft, invisible to students, until you publish it.

Correct answers never leave the server. The paper sent to a student's browser has the answer key stripped out, so it cannot be found by inspecting the page. Marking happens on the server when the exam is submitted.

Seeing results and handling makeups

Under Exams, each paper has a Results button showing every student's score, the class average, highest and lowest, and who has not started. You can open any student's marked paper.

Once a paper has closed, its results screen also shows how many students missed it, with a button to Set a makeup paper. That takes you to the exam wizard with the batch and timings already filled in, the audience set to Selected students only, and everyone who missed already ticked. You write the new questions and publish.

Students who ask for a makeup appear in the Makeup requests panel at the top of the Exams tab, with a count of how many are waiting. Each row offers Set paper or Decline. Declining asks for a reason, which the student sees.

Tab 5 — Reports

The whole class at once: overall breakdown, attendance by student, daily class attendance, exam scores for every submission, and a student summary you can export to CSV. Every chart has a Table view.

Tab 6 — Activity

A running log of every attendance action — who marked what, when, and from where. Use it to check a disputed record.

4 · The rules, in one place

The fixed behaviour behind attendance and exams.

RuleValue
Attendance button opens30 minutes before the class start time
Grace period for Present15 minutes after the start time — fixed, and the same for every batch
LateFrom the end of the grace period until the class end time
AbsentAny class day with no record, once the class has ended
Days countedOnly your batch's class days, and only from the day you joined
Marks per dayOne. It cannot be changed by the student afterwards.
Exam startAt the exact time set. A student may still begin up to 30 minutes after the paper's full length has passed.
Per-question timeSet by the teacher, minimum 5 seconds. Enforced by the server.
Attempts per examOne
Makeup requestsOne per student per missed exam
Class clockAsia/Calcutta — all times mean Indian local time, wherever the site is hosted
Password rulesAt least 8 characters, including a letter and a number
Reset linksValid 60 minutes, single use

Classes cannot run past midnight. A batch or exam window must start and end on the same calendar day. A class from 23:00 to 01:00 is not supported.

5 · If something goes wrong

The common problems and what to do about them.

What you seeWhat to do
The attendance button is greyed out It is either more than 30 minutes before class, after class has ended, or today is not one of your batch's class days. The message under the button says which.
"Choose your batch to start marking attendance" You have not picked a batch yet. Do that first — attendance cannot be recorded without a timetable.
Start exam is greyed out The exam has not opened yet. The countdown on screen shows how long is left, and the button switches on by itself.
"Time is up for this question" That question's countdown finished. The answer you gave in time has been kept. Move on to the next question.
A student says their attendance is wrong Teacher: open Today, find them, and use Set status. Check the Activity tab first to see what was actually recorded.
An uploaded paper came out wrong The review screen shows warnings and the raw extracted text. Fix the questions by hand there, or upload a cleaner file. Nothing is saved until you publish.
The page says the server is not set up Administrator: open /api/health on the site. It names exactly what is missing.
