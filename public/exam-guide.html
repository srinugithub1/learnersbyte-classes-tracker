How to write your online exam

Learner's Byte · in association with Udayan Care. Read this once before your first paper — it takes three minutes and explains the one thing students are usually caught out by: the timer on each question.

The short version. Open the Online Exam tab → tap your paper → read the details → Start exam → answer each question before its timer runs out → Submit exam. Your score appears straight away.

1 Find your exams

Log in to the student portal and tap Online Exam along the top. This is where every paper released to your batch appears.

Start exam

The five facts, your teacher's instructions, and the Start button.

The Start exam button is greyed out until the paper opens, with a live countdown beside it. Wait on this screen and it turns blue by itself at the start time — you do not need to reload.

Press Start only when you are ready to sit the whole paper. The clock begins the moment you press it, and you get one attempt only. Sit somewhere quiet, with a charged device and a steady internet connection, before you press it.

The numbers along the bottom

13 plain not opened yet. Its timer has not started.

Tap any number to jump straight to that question.

There is no Save button. Your answer is stored the moment you choose an option. You will never lose an answer by moving to the next question.

5 The timer rule — read this twice

Each question's timer starts the first time you open that question, and it does not stop. When it reaches zero:

  • Whatever you have chosen is kept and marked.
  • The question locks. You may still look at it, but you cannot change it.
  • Coming back later does not give you more time.

A question you have not opened yet still has its full time waiting — so skipping ahead does not waste it. But once you have looked, the clock is running whether you answer or not.

Changing your device's clock does nothing. The timing is decided by the server, not by your phone or laptop. If the two ever disagree, the server wins and the question locks.

6 Finish and get your score

There are two ways a paper ends.

You press Submit

Submit exam appears in place of Next on the last question. You will be asked to confirm, and told how many questions are still unanswered.

The exam end time arrives

At the finish time the paper is submitted and marked automatically, with whatever you have answered so far. You do not need to be watching — this happens even if your battery dies or your internet drops.

7 The rules, in one place

  1. One attempt only. Once you start, you cannot start again.
  2. Pick one option per question. These papers are multiple choice.
  3. No negative marking. A wrong answer costs the same as no answer, so answer every question — never leave one blank.
  4. Do not refresh or close the page. Both clocks keep running.
  5. Work on your own. Your teacher can see when every answer was given.
  6. Answers save themselves. There is no Save button.

8 If something goes wrong

What happenedWhat to do
My internet dropped mid-exam Reconnect and open the paper again — it says In progress and you carry on from where you were. Both clocks kept running, so go straight back to answering.
I closed the page by mistake The same — reopen it from the Online Exam tab. Your saved answers are all still there.
A question locked before I answered That question is finished and counts as unanswered. Move on — do not spend the next question's time worrying about it.
I missed the exam completely Open the paper. If it shows Missed, there is a Request to write button. Give your reason and your teacher decides. If approved, a makeup paper appears in your list marked ★ Makeup paper.
The Start button will not turn on The paper has not opened yet — the countdown beside it shows how long is left. Stay on the screen; it enables itself.
My score looks wrong Open Review answers to see each question marked. If you still think there is a mistake, show your teacher — they can see the same paper.

9 Before you press Start

  • Battery above 50%, or plugged in.
  • Steady internet — mobile data is fine, weak signal is not.
  • A quiet place where you will not be interrupted for the full paper.
  • Other apps and tabs closed.
  • You know the finish time, and you have enough time before it.
Stuck on something this page does not answer? Ask your teacher before the exam day — not five minutes before the paper opens.
